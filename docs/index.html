An open identification key to the wild plants of Britain and Ireland. Step 1 finds the family; step 2 finds the species. Pick any features you can see, in any order. Best matches come first, so one wrong answer won't hide the right plant.

Data. Names, status, heights, Ellenberg wetness and hectad counts come from Kew's BIFloraExplorer (Henniges et al. 2022, Scientific Data 9:1, MIT/OGL). "Where is it growing?" for families is calculated from those Ellenberg values. All family and species features are unverified drafts written by Claude from general botanical knowledge, not copied from a published flora. Check any identification against Stace or another flora. Heights marked * are estimates where Kew has no value.

Install. Android/Chrome: menu, then Install app. iPhone: Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Once installed it works with no signal.
