Prototype: mint and dead-nettle family. A multi-access key to 20 common British and Irish members of the mint family (Lamiaceae): square stems, opposite leaves, two-lipped flowers. Pick any features you can see, in any order. Species are ranked by how many of your answers they contradict, so one wrong answer won't hide the right plant.

Data. Names, status, maximum height, Ellenberg values and hectad counts come from Kew's BIFloraExplorer (Henniges et al. 2022, Scientific Data 9:1, MIT/OGL), joined on Kew ID. Morphological scores and tips are unverified drafts written by Claude from general botanical knowledge, not copied from a published flora. Check any identification against Stace or another flora. Heights marked * are estimates where Kew has no value.

Install. Android/Chrome: menu, then Install app. iPhone: Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Once installed it works with no signal.
